fm/dm impulse responses

Recorded spaces (*.flac in this folder) are from Voxengo's free reverb impulse responses,
https://www.voxengo.com/impulses/ (via https://github.com/oramics/sampled/tree/master/IR/Voxengo),
converted to FLAC, the longest trimmed to 6 seconds. Their licence:
"Permission is granted to anyone to use these impulse files royalty-free for any purpose,
including commercial usage."

The other factory impulse responses (room, studio, chamber, plate, hall, cathedral, spring,
gated, reverse, tin can) are synthesised by fm/dm itself (src/ui/ir.js).
